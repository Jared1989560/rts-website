Who We Are

Rogue Technical Services LLC is an independent E&I installation, commissioning, QAQC, and reliability services firm built for industrial and energy projects. We are not a staffing agency, and we are not a large contractor trying to win scope we can't execute. We are a focused technical team that does E&I work — from the first conduit run to final turnover — and we're proud of it.

We are based in Houston, TX and work projects across the United States. Our team has direct field experience across natural gas compression and transmission, pipeline facilities, LNG, biofuels, and heavy industrial sites. When we send someone to your project, they know the equipment, they know the documentation, and they know what's expected at turnover.

Whether you need a full E&I installation crew, a commissioning team, owner's QAQC representation, or ongoing reliability support — Rogue can deliver each service independently or as a coordinated scope. You don't have to choose between installation quality and commissioning readiness when the same team handles both.

Our Commitment

We built Rogue around one straightforward idea: do the work right, document it properly, and stand behind it. That applies whether we're pulling wire on a new greenfield station or troubleshooting a reliability issue at an operating facility that's been running for a decade.

We don't oversell and underdeliver. If a project scope isn't a fit, we'll tell you — and point you toward someone who can help. If it is a fit, we'll give you a straight quote, a realistic timeline, and technicians who know what they're doing when they show up.

That's why clients keep coming back. Not because we're the cheapest, and not because we're the biggest — because we show up, we deliver, and the paperwork is clean at turnover.

Safety, Compliance & Insurance

We know the first thing a contractor or operator's safety team checks before approving a vendor. Here's what you need:

Safety Program

  • OSHA 10 and OSHA 30 certified field personnel
  • Written safety program — LOTO, hot work, confined space, fall protection
  • JSA (Job Safety Analysis) completed before every task
  • Stop-Work Authority — every person on the crew, no exceptions
  • H&S documentation available upon request for pre-qualification

Insurance & Compliance

  • Current Certificate of Insurance (COI) available on request
  • General liability and workers' compensation maintained
  • Additional insured endorsements available for project requirements
  • Drug-free workplace policy — pre-employment and random screening
  • Able to meet site-specific safety requirements for refineries, LNG, and gas facilities
